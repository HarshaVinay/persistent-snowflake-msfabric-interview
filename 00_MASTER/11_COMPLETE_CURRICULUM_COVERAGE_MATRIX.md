## Purpose

## Priority definitions
- **P0:** explain clearly, code where relevant, troubleshoot, and defend in interview.
- **P1:** explain and solve normal interview questions; deepen after P0.
- **P2:** know the definition, purpose, and one example unless the interviewer probes deeper.

## Notes on terminology
- The pasted curriculum says **“CID Properties”** in the transactions section. This repository treats that as a terminology item to verify against the training material before finalizing the exact expected wording; do not silently assume whether the trainer intended ACID.
- The pasted curriculum says **“RUNCATE”** in one place; the standard SQL command is `TRUNCATE`. The repository uses `TRUNCATE` in technical explanations.

## Completion rule
Every curriculum item must be covered by at least one canonical master note and represented in final-review material. P0 topics additionally require coding/scenario practice.
