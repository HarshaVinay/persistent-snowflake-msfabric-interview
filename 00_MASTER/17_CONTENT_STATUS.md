## Definition of done
A topic is **content-complete** when the relevant master note covers the curriculum, core concepts, practical examples, interview answer, follow-ups, common mistakes, and a self-test.

A topic is **interview-ready** only after the user can answer and code it without notes and survive at least two follow-up questions.

## Current bottleneck
The repository's structure is no longer the bottleneck. The bottleneck is active practice and recall.
