# Evidence Rules

## A — VERIFIED / DIRECTLY REPORTED
Candidate explicitly reports being asked the question at Persistent Systems.

## B — REPORTED, EXPERIENCED-LEVEL
Question is reported at Persistent but mainly comes from experienced candidates. Useful for deeper preparation, but do not assume the same depth for a fresher.

## C — HIGH-PROBABILITY PREDICTION
Not directly confirmed as asked, but strongly supported by overlap between the user's curriculum, current Persistent role requirements, and repeated interview patterns.

## D — GENERAL DATA ENGINEERING
Useful knowledge that is not supported as Persistent-specific interview evidence.

## Rules
- Never present C or D as a confirmed Persistent question.
- Never merge senior-candidate expectations into fresher expectations without labeling them.
- Prefer primary candidate reports over generic interview lists.
- When a source is inaccessible, do not claim to have read its full contents.
- If multiple independent candidate reports contain the same question, label it **Repeated Across Multiple Reports**.
- If supported by only one candidate report, label it **Single Report**.
